Privacy Policy

EcomData-IQ. Last updated: October 22, 2026.

EcomData-IQ provides data analytics and reporting services to e-commerce brands. Our internal reporting applications connect, with the account owner's authorization, to advertising and analytics platforms such as Meta (Facebook) Marketing API, Google Ads, Google Analytics, Pinterest and Shopify. This policy explains what data these applications access and how it is handled.

1. Data we access

Only advertising and business performance data from accounts our clients have authorized us to read, for example campaign names, daily spend, impressions, clicks, conversions and conversion value. Our applications use read-only access. We do not access, collect or store personal data of individual platform users (for example Pinners or Facebook users), and we do not read Pins, boards or posts.

2. How we use the data

Strictly for analytics and reporting for the client who owns the account: dashboards, performance analysis and answering the client's business questions. We do not use the data for advertising, profiling individuals or any other purpose.

3. Storage and security

Data is stored in the client's private analytics environment (a private database and data warehouse with access limited to EcomData-IQ and the client's authorized users). Access tokens and credentials are stored privately and never published or shared.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties. Data is only shown to the client who owns it and the people that client authorizes.

5. Consent and revoking access

Access is granted by the account owner or administrator through the platform's own authorization screen. Access can be revoked at any time in the platform's settings (for example Pinterest: Settings > Security and logins > Apps) or by contacting us.

6. Retention and deletion

Data is kept for as long as we provide services to the client. On request, or when the engagement ends, we delete the client's data and revoke our access within 30 days.
